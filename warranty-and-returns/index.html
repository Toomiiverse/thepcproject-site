Warranty & Returns

Every system is tested before handover. This warranty sits on top of your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

What’s included

  • 30 days - we make sure the system is fully functional from day one.
  • 12 months complimentary tech support - for the individual who bought the system.
  • 3 years hardware coverage on pre-built and custom systems, at The PC Project’s discretion, for defects in hardware and workmanship under normal use.

Level-Up program

An optional add-on: $100 buys three years of upgrades with no service fees.

  • Trade in any new part that was in your build - CPU, GPU, motherboard or RAM.
  • We value the part at market rate and put that toward the upgrade.
  • No service fee on the swap. A GPU swap would normally cost $50.
  • Runs for three years from purchase.

Full detail on the Level-Up page.

Warranty periods

Cover starts on the date of original retail purchase on your invoice.

  • Three (3) years - all new parts procured directly from manufacturers, resellers and distributors
  • One (1) year - tech support for the buying individual
  • Ninety (90) days - QA Approved components (CPU, GPU, Motherboard, RAM)
  • Thirty (30) days - general warranty for all refurbished parts sourced by The PC Project and used in any sales or builds
  • Thirty (30) days - workmanship on repairs, upgrades and servicing

What it covers

This warranty applies to products supplied and assembled by The PC Project. Custom and pre-built systems are return-to-base: we diagnose and repair hardware faults under normal use. Individual components follow the manufacturer’s warranty where one applies.

It does not cover physical or accidental damage, misuse, neglect, unauthorised repairs or modifications, software issues (unless we specified otherwise), or damage in transit from poor return packaging. Repairing or modifying the system without our approval may void cover.

QA Approved components

Pre-owned, trade-in or customer-supplied parts go through inspection, testing, and sign-off before they go into a PC build. Only parts that pass our 4-stage assessment can be used. They are covered by a 90-day limited performance warranty: verified function at delivery and stability under normal use. Cosmetic wear, misuse, overclocking, external damage, and failures outside a reasonable lifespan are not included.

Full detail on the QA Assessed page.

Repairs, upgrades & servicing

Bench work - CPU repastes, GPU and other part swaps, cleaning, upgrades and diagnostics - carries a thirty-day workmanship warranty. If something we did is not right, bring it back and we will put it right at no charge.

It covers our work, not the hardware itself. A part that was already faulty, aged or damaged before it reached us is not covered, and neither is a new fault unrelated to what we did. Parts you supplied are covered by their own manufacturer warranty. Terms for servicing are on the Services page.

Claims

Email support@thepcproject.com.au. We diagnose, talk you through troubleshooting, and arrange a return if needed. If a fault is confirmed we repair, replace, or otherwise resolve it under this warranty.

Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have goods repaired or replaced if they fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure is not a major failure.
