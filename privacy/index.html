Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we do with it. We keep it to what the job needs and nothing more. This sits under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Who we are

The PC Project, 2/62 Fitzgerald Street, Northbridge WA 6003. When we say “we” or “us” below, that is who you are dealing with.

What we collect

  • Contact details - name, email, and phone number when you request a quote, book a build or a service, or get in touch.
  • Build details - the answers and parts from a configurator quote, and what you tell us about how the machine will be used.
  • Service details - the machine you bring in and the fault or work requested. We do not read or copy your files, and we ask you to back up your own data before a service.
  • Payment - handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.

Why we collect it

To quote, build, service and support your order, to answer your enquiry, and to keep a record of the work for your warranty. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

Only where the job needs it - for example our payment provider to take payment, or a courier to deliver a machine. We do not disclose your information to anyone else unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

This website

We do not run tracking or advertising cookies here. The maps on this site are Google Maps embeds, and the Instagram and Facebook links go to those services - each handles your data under its own policy. The configurator runs on our own shop systems so we can turn a spec into a quote.

How we store it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the job and for the records we are required to hold, then dispose of it securely. We take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email us and we will sort it out.

Contact

contact@thepcproject.com.au · 2/62 Fitzgerald Street, Northbridge WA 6003. If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy matter, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
