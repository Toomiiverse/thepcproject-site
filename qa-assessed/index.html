Tested-&-Traceable.//

From Trade-In
To Trusted;

Every pre-owned part we sell passes a structured 4-stage QA process before it's approved for resale or a build. This is what happens between a part walking in and a part earning the sticker. Scroll.

↓
01
01 Procurement.//

It Arrives

Every part starts as a question mark. A trade-in, a bulk lot, a card someone swears "ran fine". It gets logged into inventory with its source and identifying information — traceable from day one.

  • Source-Logged
  • Serial-Recorded
  • Inventory-ID
02
02 Assessment.//

It Gets Documented

Condition is inspected and photographed before anything is powered on. Visible damage, missing components, corrosion, wear — all of it goes on record, not under the rug.

  • Photographed
  • Damage-Check
  • Corrosion
  • Wear
03
03 Testing.//

It Gets Pushed

Now it powers on and earns its keep. Appropriate workloads verify functionality, stability, and performance — benchmarks, stress tests, thermal behaviour. Numbers, not vibes.

  • Benchmarked
  • Stress-Tested
  • Thermals
  • Stability
04
04 Sign-Off.//

The Verdict

Results are recorded and the item is formally marked QA Approved, rejected, or flagged for further investigation. Only approved parts reach the shelf or go into a build.

  • Approved
  • Rejected
  • Investigate

The-Whole-Point.//

QA-Approved;

Used hardware becomes documented, traceable inventory — not a dusty GPU with a “tested” sticker.
