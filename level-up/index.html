How-It-Works.//

Four Steps

  1. 01 Opt-In.//

    Add It To Your Build

    A flat $100 on top of your build, taken at purchase. It runs for three years from that date.

  2. 02 Trade.//

    Bring The Part Back

    Any new part that was in your build - CPU, GPU, motherboard or RAM. Bring it in whenever you are ready to move up.

  3. 03 Value.//

    Priced At Market

    We value the part at what it is actually worth on the day, and put that straight toward the upgrade.

  4. 04 Swap.//

    No Service Fee

    We do the swap and test the machine, and you pay nothing for the labour. A GPU swap would normally cost $50.

What-You-Can-Trade.//

Four Parts

  • CPU
  • GPU
  • Motherboard
  • RAM

It applies to new parts that were in your original build. QA Approved and customer-supplied parts are covered by their own warranty instead - see Warranty & Returns.
