On-The-Bench.//

Services

Repastes, part swaps, cleaning, upgrades and repairs - on any machine, not just the ones we built. We assess it and quote before anything is touched.

What-We-Do.//

On The Bench

CPU-Repaste

Old paste comes off, the cooler is cleaned and reseated with fresh compound. The usual fix for a chip running hotter and louder than it used to.

GPU-Swap

$50

Out with the old card, in with the new. We check the supply and clearances first, fit it, and test the machine before it goes back to you.

PC-Clean

Dust out of the filters, fans, radiator and heatsinks, cables tidied on the way through. Airflow back to what it was on day one.

Upgrades

Memory, storage, cooling, a bigger supply. We check the new part suits the board and the case before anything is ordered.

Diagnostics

It will not boot, it crashes, it throttles, it is louder than it should be. We find out why and tell you what it will cost to fix.

Repairs

Faulty parts identified, replaced and tested. If it is under a manufacturer warranty we will tell you rather than charge you for it.

Not listed? Bring it in anyway. If it is something we cannot do, we will say so and point you somewhere that can.

The-Fine-Print.//

Service Terms

  • Back up your data first. Servicing can require a reinstall or a drive swap. We are not responsible for data lost during a service, so please back up anything you cannot replace before you leave the machine with us.
  • Aged and faulty hardware. A part that is already failing, worn or damaged can give out during or shortly after a service. We will tell you when we can see the risk, but we cannot guarantee the outcome on hardware that was already on its way out.
  • Parts you supply. We will fit them, but they are fitted at your risk and covered by their own manufacturer warranty rather than ours.
  • Approval before work. Nothing is touched until you have the price and have said yes. If the job grows once it is open, we stop and re-quote.
  • Diagnostics. Some faults take time to find. Where a fault cannot be reproduced or traced, we will tell you what we have ruled out rather than guess at a repair.
  • Collection. Please collect finished machines within 30 days. After that we may charge storage.
  • Workmanship. Our work is covered for thirty days - see Warranty & Returns. It covers what we did, not the hardware itself.
