How-It-Works.//

The Process

  1. 01 Consult.//

    Talk It Through

    Come in-store, or start with our online configurator. We work out what the machine is actually for, and tailor the build to suit.

  2. 02 Design.//

    Plan The Build

    We research the best parts and deals inside your budget, then handle all procurement. You approve the final spec before the build begins.

  3. 03 Build.//

    Attention To Detail

    Assembled around what you need most, in the style you want, at the performance you are paying for - cable management and thermals included, not extra.

  4. 04 Deliver.//

    Tested & Warrantied

    Every build is benchmarked before handover and covered by our Zero-Failure Initiative warranty. Local delivery as far as Bunbury.

QA-Assessed-Parts

Want more machine for the money? We can build with QA Assessed parts - pre-owned hardware put through our four-stage assessment and covered for ninety days. See how a part earns the sticker.

Level-Up-Program

Add it to your build for $100 and, for three years, trade your CPU, GPU, motherboard or RAM back at market value toward the next one - with no service fee on the swap. Read the program.
